Product Matching Evaluation

This evaluation will test your ability to match a product to the correct menu items from various retailers.

How to Match:

You will be shown a product from our catalog and asked to identify which menu items from retailers match this product.

  • Review the Product: Look at the product details including:
    • Product Name and Brand
    • Description and Category
    • Available Regions
    • Image Text (if available)
  • Evaluate Menu Items: For each menu item option, consider:
    • Brand Match: Does the menu item brand match the product brand?
    • Product Match: Does the menu item name/description represent the same product?
    • Variant Match: Do the specific details (weight, potency, count, etc.) match?
    • Region Compatibility: Is the menu item from a region where the product is available?
  • Select Matches: Choose ALL menu items that match the given product. You can select multiple items if they all represent the same product in different regions or with equivalent details.
  • None of the Above: If no menu items match the product, select "None of the above" as your only choice.
